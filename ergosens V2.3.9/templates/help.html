Multi-Modal Architecture

ErgoSense combines physical telemetry and simulated environmental stress data into a unified, privacy-first fatigue framework.

1

Machine Learning Posture Framework

Utilizes MediaPipe to extract human pose landmarks in real-time. Features are engineered into geometric relationships (neck angle, spinal distance) and fed into our trained Scikit-Learn model to classify "Optimal" or "Strained" states. Data is securely logged to a local SQLite database, and raw camera frames are discarded instantly for privacy.

2

Visual Strain Simulation

Prolonged screen time leads to digital eye strain. ErgoSense simulates this by tracking continuous operational hours, incrementally raising the visual stress coefficient. This data is modeled dynamically on the backend and pushed to the dashboard to represent likely visual fatigue.

3

Vocal Stress Simulation

Meetings and continuous communication heavily impact overall fatigue. Our architecture mocks vocal stress spikes during traditional daily meeting hours (e.g., mid-morning and mid-afternoon) to provide a holistic view of occupational friction beyond physical posture.

4

Drowsiness Monitor & Aggregation

Simulating circadian rhythm dips, the drowsiness index tracks general wakefulness. Finally, the ErgoSense Aggregation Engine applies weighted calculations to all four parameters to predict the precise hour of the day where user productivity is maximized and physiological stress is lowest.
